Push Button Management

Run your day, instead of your day running you.

A simple system built on Gmail labels and Stream Deck buttons. One press, and a message is owned, filed, and out of your way.

Your inbox sorts by time. Your work doesn’t.

  • Every tool you use shows you what came in last, not what matters most.
  • So you check, refresh, and make the same small decisions all day.

What’s inside

The system in three ideas

  1. 1

    Labels, not folders

    Tag a message with who owns it, then archive it. When you can pull it back in a second, it doesn’t need to sit in front of you.

  2. 2

    Buttons are decisions

    Buttons are organized by the kind of work you’re doing, not by app. Your hand learns where to go.

  3. 3

    One owner per item

    Every item gets one responsible person. Others can help, but everyone knows who has the ball.
