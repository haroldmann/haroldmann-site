What’s inside

The system in three ideas

  1. 1

    Labels, not folders

    Tag a message with who owns it, then archive it. When you can pull it back in a second, it doesn’t need to sit in front of you.

  2. 2

    Buttons are decisions

    Buttons are organized by the kind of work you’re doing, not by app. Your hand learns where to go.

  3. 3

    One owner per item

    Every item gets one responsible person. Others can help, but everyone knows who has the ball.
