What’s inside

The system in three ideas

  1. 1

    Labels, not folders

    Tag messages by owner: they archive and return only when you need them.

  2. 2

    Buttons are decisions

    Buttons are organized by the kind of work you’re doing, not by app. Build your own muscle memory based on how you like to manage.

  3. 3

    Have meetings prep automatically

    Combine summaries, lists and meeting notes into a single button press.
