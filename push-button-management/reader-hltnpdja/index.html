Icon prompts 20

Global style prompt

Paste first, every time.

Create a single flat, strictly monochromatic icon for a small square hardware button (an Elgato Stream Deck key). Use exactly one ink color, solid black, on a plain pure-white background, with no other colors, tints, or shades anywhere. High contrast, bold and uniform stroke weight, clean geometric lines. No gradients, no shadows, no glow, no 3D, no bevels, no texture, no reflections, no borders, and no background scenery. Keep it minimal and focused: one clear central subject, generous empty margins, and no decorative flourishes, extra detail, or ornamentation. The icon must stay instantly legible when shrunk to about 72 pixels square. Square 1:1 composition, centered.

Subject lines

Assignment (#GMS)

Subject: the text “#GMS” only, in a heavy uppercase sans-serif, solid black, sized to fill most of the square with even margins and without touching the edges. No icon, no underline, no box, no other graphics, just the bold lettering.

Weekly Dashboard

Subject: a minimal calendar suggested by a plain square divided into a few even cells, with one small check mark in a single corner. Solid black line work of uniform stroke weight. No numbers, no text, and no extra marks.

Time-Shift (Alarm)

Subject: a simple alarm clock viewed straight on, with two small bells on top and a plain round face, drawn in solid black with uniform strokes, centered. No numbers, no shading, no background, and only a simple pair of hands at most.

Delegation

Subject: one bold right-pointing arrow that signals handoff, solid black, with a thick uniform stroke, centered and well inside the margins. Just the single arrow, with no second arrow, no box, no text, and nothing else.

You are preparing me for a first sales conversation. Using only public information, give me a one-page briefing on the company or person below: what they do, size and stage, recent news from the last 12 months, likely priorities for someone in their role, and three specific questions I could ask that show I did my homework. Flag anything you are unsure of instead of guessing. Keep it under 300 words.

Should this be a button?

Any time: describe a task you repeat.

Here is a task I repeat at work: [describe it]. Apply three tests: does it repeat at least three times a week, is it multi-step, and does it cause friction or hesitation? If all three are true, write the exact Stream Deck Multi Action steps for it (Hotkey, Text, Delay, Website), with a 200 to 300 ms delay after anything that opens a menu or window.
